1. About this tool

"Cooper Universe Publisher" is a private tool created and used only by the owner of the @cooperversetv accounts (Cooper Universe). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

After the owner signs in and approves it, the tool reads the public statistics of the owner's own TikTok videos (for example views, likes and comments). It does not read or collect other people's accounts. It can also upload the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own TikTok account, only after the owner approves each post.

The tool is used only by the owner, for the owner's own account, and in line with the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Developer Terms. Nothing is posted, deleted or changed on any account by the tool unless the owner clearly approves it first.

All videos and texts belong to their owner. Some videos use short official clips and images from film trailers for commentary and news purposes. Videos that use AI are labeled where the platform asks.

6. Ending

The owner can stop using the tool and disconnect it from TikTok at any time. We can end the tool at any time.

7. Privacy

How data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy of Cooper Universe.

8. Contact

Send a message to @cooperversetv on Instagram.

9. Changes

If these terms change, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
